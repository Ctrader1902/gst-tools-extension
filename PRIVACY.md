# GST Tools — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

GST Tools is a Chrome extension published by Asif & Associates. It helps you
download your own GST returns from the GST portal.

## What the extension collects

Nothing. GST Tools does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any
personal or business data. It has no analytics and makes no network requests
of its own.

## What stays on your computer

- The files you download, saved in your Downloads\GST Downloads folder.
- Your last choices in the panel (financial year, return type, months) and the
  activity log, kept in Chrome's local extension storage.
- When a month fails, a short technical note about the portal page (its
  address, title, visible message and button names) is kept locally so you
  can press "Copy debug info". It is only shared if you paste it to someone
  yourself.

The extension never reads, stores or sends your GST password or captcha: you
log in to the portal yourself.

## Permissions and why they are needed

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Access to GST portal sites (*.gst.gov.in) | To click through the portal pages (returns, GSTR-2B) in the tab the extension opens for a run |
| downloads | To save and name the downloaded return files |
| storage | To remember your choices and the activity log |
| scripting | To run the page helper inside the run's portal tab |
| sidePanel | To show the GST Tools panel |
| contentSettings | During a run only, to let GST portal sites (*.gst.gov.in) download several files in a row (GSTR-2B has more than one file); the setting is removed when the run ends |
